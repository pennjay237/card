### Project Title
card flipper game 

## How it works
- click on the start button to start the game.
- click on 2 cards, 1 after the other to match them.
- If they macth they stay flipped but if not, they turn back.

## Key Features

-Typically in a 4x4 or 6x6 grid.

-Each card has a front and a back (hidden face).

-Clicking a card flips it to reveal the symbol/image underneath.

-Flip animations use CSS transitions or 3D transforms.

-Players flip two cards at a time:

-If the symbols/images match → both cards remain visible.

-If not → both cards flip back after a short delay.

-Allows users to reshuffle and restart the game.
